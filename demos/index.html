What We Do

Comprehensive Plumbing Services

From urgent middle-of-the-night leaks to planned kitchen upgrades, our experienced Chattanooga crew handles it all.

24/7 Emergency Leaks

Burst pipes, sudden pressure drops, and severe water leaks diagnosed and isolated rapidly to prevent structural water damage.

  • Immediate dispatch capability
  • Pipe line isolation & repair
  • Emergency valve replacement

Faucet & Fixture Install

Complete installation and repairs for kitchen, bath, and utility faucets. We pick up replacement fixtures or install your custom pick.

  • Kitchen sink & faucet upgrades
  • Showerhead & valve replacement
  • Drip & seal repairs

Garbage Disposals

Jammed, humming, or burnt-out disposal units fixed in 30 minutes or less. Clean, reliable replacement units carried on truck.

  • Fast 30-minute turnarounds
  • Jam clearing & motor reset
  • Heavy-duty unit installation

Drain & Pipe Unclogging

Clearing stubborn backups in sinks, toilets, and main drain lines to restore smooth flow throughout your residence.

  • Main line obstruction removal
  • Kitchen & bathroom drain snaking
  • Preventative pipe flush

Water Heater Maintenance

Diagnostic and component replacements for tank and tankless water heaters so you never have to deal with cold showers.

  • Heating element replacement
  • Thermostat & valve checks
  • Sediment flush & tune-up

Residential Plumbing Inspection

Comprehensive plumbing checkups for home buyers, military families, or general maintenance across Hamilton County.

  • Full pipe integrity checks
