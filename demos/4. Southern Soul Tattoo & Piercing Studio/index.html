About Southern Soul

A Studio Built on Respect & Real Artistry

At Southern Soul Tattoo & Piercing Studio, we believe getting body art should be a memorable, positive experience from start to finish. Located on Ashland Terrace in Chattanooga, our space is meticulously clean, bright, and staffed by experienced artists who treat your ideas with the skill they deserve.

Whether you are adding to a sleeve, getting custom artwork designed from scratch, or bringing a family member in for a professional piercing, Samantha, DC, and the entire Southern Soul team ensure every client feels heard, respected, and expertly cared for.
