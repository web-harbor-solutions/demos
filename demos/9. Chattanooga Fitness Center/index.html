Public Amenity in Chattanooga

An Incredible Free Resource for All Chattanoogans

"Such an amazing free resource here in Chatt. Gym has always felt very comfy inside even on hot or cold days and everyone is very friendly and respectful."

Chattanooga Fitness Center is one of our city’s finest public amenities—providing high-caliber gym equipment, spacious functional movement areas, and expert group classes completely free to the community.

Whether you are squeezing in a quick workout right after work, training heavy with free weights, or joining neighbors for lighthearted dance fitness, you’ll find a clean, comfortable, and respectful environment every time you step through our doors.

Never Over-Crowded

Spacious floor plan ensures you get access to machines and racks without waiting in long lines.

Climate Controlled Comfort

Consistently comfortable indoor environment so you can train hard regardless of weather outside.
