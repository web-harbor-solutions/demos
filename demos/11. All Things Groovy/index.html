For years, All Things Groovy has been a cherished fixture on Frazier Avenue. Owned and operated by Lee, our store is a sanctuary of vibrant color, calming aromatics, and unique hand-picked finds.

Whether you are looking for soothing incense to elevate your home, searching for a meaningful crystal or dreamcatcher gift, or simply browsing fun pins, stickers, and boho fashion—you’ll always feel warmly welcomed here.
