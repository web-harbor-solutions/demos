Meet The Salon

Personalized Care by Mattie

At Urban Dog, we treat your pet like one of our own. Located in Suite 126 at 804 Riverfront Parkway, our boutique salon was designed to feel calm, quiet, and friendly—a welcome contrast to chaotic high-volume grooming chains.

From severe coat de-matting for newly adopted rescues to routine 5-week trims for Goldendoodles, Mini Doodles, and all breeds, Mattie and the Urban Dog team deliver top-notch styling with zero rush and maximum affection.
