Why Chattanooga Pet Parents Trust Jess & Her Team

"We followed her for many years and we were so happy for her when she opened Hairy Styles. I trust my poodle mix and golden retriever will always be well taken care of and groomed to perfection."

Hairy Styles Pet Grooming was founded on a simple principle: every pet deserves to be treated with patience, kindness, and meticulous care. Conveniently located on St Elmo Ave (just down the block from Buchanan's Barber Lounge), Jess and her team have built a loyal following across Chattanooga.

Whether your pup needs a light trim, coat deshedding, or a special medicated bath, we work efficiently (most grooming turnarounds under 2 hours) while maintaining an unbeatable, affordable rate structure.
